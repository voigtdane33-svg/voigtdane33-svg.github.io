About

I am a science teacher at Belleville West High School in Southern Illinois, where I teach biology/ecology and human biology. I have been teaching science for three years and currently teach the ecology concepts highlighted on this website. I design much of my own instructional material and focus on making science accessible, understandable, and connected to students’ everyday lives. My interest in instructional design and education technology has influenced how I create and present content for students with different learning needs.

Education and Background

I earned a bachelor’s degree in social science from Troy University and a master's degree in secondary education from Mississippi State University. I am currently pursuing an Educational Specialist (EdS) in Learning Technologies and Design with the University of Missouri to continue improving my instructional design skills and the way I use technology to support learning.
